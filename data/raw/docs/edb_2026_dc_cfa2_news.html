Four data centre proposals selected as part of second data centre - call for application (“DC-CFA2”)

21 Aug 2026•1 min read

The Singapore Economic Development Board (EDB) and the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA) provisionally allocated 200 megawatts (MW) of new capacity to four data centre (DC) operators through the Second Data Centre – Call for Application (DC-CFA2) exercise.
